# Competitions: The Shortcut to Credibility

Competition results are demonstrated ability you can put on a resume — sometimes a
direct line to a recruiter.

## Numerai
A hedge fund that crowdsources trading signals. You submit predictions, they trade
on them, you earn crypto payouts if you're good. Free, real stakes, and a good
Numerai track record is legitimately impressive to employers. Top performers on
the leaderboard sometimes get recruited directly.

## QuantConnect
Open-source algorithmic-trading platform that runs algorithm competitions. A
winning entry goes on the resume. Also has a job board with a direct line to firms
that hire algorithmic traders.

## Kaggle Financial Datasets
Not finance-specific, but Kaggle's prediction competitions build your ML muscles
and are good public evidence of modeling skill.
