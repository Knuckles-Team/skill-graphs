# Certifications That Actually Matter (Phase 6)

Degrees matter less than they used to in quant, but credentials still open doors.

## Certificate in Quantitative Finance (CQF)
The most respected professional certification in quant finance. 6 months, online,
**£15,000**. Worth it if you're serious and can afford it. Taught by practitioners,
not academics.

## CFA Level 1
Foundational finance knowledge. Respected at banks and asset managers. ~6 months
of study, exam ~**$900**. Less relevant for pure quant roles, more relevant for
risk and portfolio management.

## QuantInsti's EPAT
Executive Programme in Algorithmic Trading. Cheaper than CQF, more focused on
implementation. Good for entry-level quant-developer roles at smaller firms.

## Free / audit options
- **Coursera: Machine Learning Specialization (Andrew Ng)** — free to audit; the
  most-watched ML course ever made. Do this *before* you touch financial ML.
- **MIT OpenCourseWare 18.S096** — *Topics in Mathematics with Applications in
  Finance*. The actual MIT course. Free, rigorous — master's-level material.
